Who We Are

More than a brand — a collective

OBI started as a handful of people who were tired of watching women get talked down to about their own bodies. That group has grown — a small core team, a wider circle of practitioners and contributors, and a network of partner organizations who share our conviction that good health information should be plain, honest and usable.

Everyone in this collective, whether they draw a paycheck from OBI or simply believe in what we're building, is here for the same reason: to close the gap between what science shows and what most women are told.
